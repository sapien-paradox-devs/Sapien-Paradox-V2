The Jewel Surface

One stylesheet is the source of truth, four colours with two shades each, and nothing glows.

SurfaceD54D74D81partly built

Why it exists

The first surface was warm cream paper, a soft display serif and a burnt-sienna accent. The owner's verdict was that it looked like Claude's own design — and a product wearing someone else's look is harder to remember as its own. D74 replaced the palette and the type; D81 replaced the palette again the same day, on a clearer brief: bring back the warm near-black, use bright colours, and use more than two.

What survived both changes is the direction underneath: depth over velocity, a book serif for anything you read at length, and nothing glows. A reader spends an hour in here.

Four colours, two shades each

What each part does

01
apps/web/src/styles/global.css
The only source of truth: ink, three papers, rules, the four colours with their washes, the two typefaces, radii, shadows, the paper grain, and the motion tokens. Vanilla CSS, no framework; component styles live beside their component.
02
global.css · the dark block
A second set of the same tokens, including a night grain of faint pale specks instead of dark ones. The CSS keys on data-theme alone, so no component needs a copy of the palette under a media query.
03
lib/theme.ts
Three preferences — system, light, dark — resolved to two painted themes. It stamps data-theme on the root, remembers the choice, and updates the browser bar colour, which is one of the copies of the palette that cannot read CSS.
04
index.html · the pre-paint script
Resolves the theme before first paint, so nobody sees a white flash on the way to dark. It cannot import theme.ts, so it mirrors the storage key and the resolve — and a test reads index.html to keep the two in step.
05
lib/motion.ts
The one place a view transition starts. It decides whether one runs and sets data-transition for its duration; what it looks like is the ::view-transition-* rules in CSS. React is flushed inside the callback, because the browser snapshots the moment it returns.
06
global.css · the transition rules
A page change gets a small rise; a theme change is a plain crossfade — the whole page moving eight pixels because the colours changed would be wrong. Reduced motion keeps the fade and drops the movement, which is gentler than a hard cut.
07
components/SiteHeader/contents.ts
What the header shows, as data: one answer per page per session state, kept apart from the component so the table is tested without a DOM. Twelve pages, including the two halves of / — the library and the landing page.
08
components/ThemeToggle/ · Skeleton/ · Spinner/
The toggle cycles the three preferences. Skeletons replaced the word "Loading" everywhere it appeared, so the first frame after a tap looks like the page that is arriving.
09
components/Button/ · TextField/ · ErrorNotice/ · Avatar/
The shared set every page was migrated onto, so a button, a field and a refusal look the same on the landing page, in the admin and in the chamber.
10
lib/labels.ts
Twenty sections, one per surface. Mandate 1 in practice: changing a word is never a change to a component.

The rules motion follows

  • Native first. CSS, the View Transitions API, scroll-linked animations. No animation library, and WebGL was rejected outright — too heavy for the phone every WhatsApp link opens on, and it breaks "nothing glows".
  • Only position, scale and opacity, so it holds 60fps on a mid-range Android phone.
  • Nothing moves while the reader is reading.
  • Dates, never countdowns.
  • Any sequence of steps is states in a machine, never a chain of timers — which is why the threshold ceremony is four states.
  • Reduced motion keeps fades and drops movement.

Gotchas

  • The palette has copies that cannot read CSS. The browser-bar colours in theme.ts, the pre-paint script and theme-color in index.html, the web manifest, the brand sources, and the watermark ink in services/pages.py. They change in the same pull request as global.css or they are simply wrong.
  • data-theme is always resolved to one of two values. "System" is a preference, not a theme — keeping the third state out of the attribute is what stops every component needing a media-query copy of its colours.
  • React has to be flushed inside the transition callback. The browser snapshots the new state the moment the callback returns, so a render still queued at that point is captured half-done.
  • A back-swipe is already animated by the browser. Running a second transition on top of hasUAVisualTransition reads as a stutter, so that case is skipped.
  • Reduced motion does not skip the transition. It becomes a short crossfade — a hard cut between two screens is harsher than the thing the setting was asking to avoid.
  • The surface stack is unmerged work. Header, link preview, dark mode, motion foundation and the opening ceremony were a five-PR stack whose Vercel previews all failed for an account reason rather than a code one, and the ordering matters: they conflict out of order.
  • Legal pages and a real 404 are still missing. Razorpay's KYC checks for the first, and today any unmatched route falls through to the library.
